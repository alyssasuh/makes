About Me

Modeling the Human Ear, 2025

Anatomical Modeling · Molding and Casting · Experimental Acoustic Characterization

A physical replica of the outer ear designed to study how sound travels through cartilage. This three-layer model—soft tissue, cartilage, and bone—recreates the ear's anatomy and material properties to simulate how vibrations applied to the pinna generate sound at the eardrum.

서 Eungyo, 2023-Present

CAD (Fusion 360) · Film photography · Optics

An ongoing exploration of analog image-making through the design and fabrication of a fully functional 35mm film camera. Built from scratch, the project investigates the mechanics of light, exposure, and optics—and how they all come together in the meditative process of photography.

REMY, 2025-2026

Prototyping · User Validation Survey · Human-Centered Design

Over 2.2 billion people globally live with visual impairment, and screen-heavy workflows cause fatigue and distraction. Remy delivers private, non-occluding audio through cartilage-mediated conduction—without sealing the ear canal.

MEMS Transducer, 2025

Photolithography · Device Assembly · PZT

A millimeter-scale transducer designed to deliver sound through the cartilage of the outer ear. Fabricated using photolithography and cleanroom MEMS processes, the device converts electrical signals into mechanical vibration via a stacked piezoelectric (PZT) architecture.

Cell Engineering, 2025

Micropatterned PDMS Stamps · HUVECs · HNDFs · Live-cell Microscopy

An exploration of how geometry and physical alignment shape collective cell behavior. By engineering defined gap geometries and anisotropic protein patterns, this project investigates how dermal fibroblasts and endothelial cells migrate and coordinate in vitro enviornments.

Mrs. Potatohead Box, 2024

CAD (Fusion 360) · Laser-cut Cardboard

A character-inspired storage box designed through 2D CAD and fabricated using laser cutting. Originally conceived with interchangeable facial features—ears, nose, lips, and feet—the project explored how flat vector sketches translate into interlocking three-dimensional form. Through iterative scaling and prototyping, the final design embraces simplicity, retaining the playful geometry of the original concept while refining proportion, fit, and fabrication constraints.

Co-contraction Activated Extendo Arm, 2023

EMG Signal Processing · Rack-and-Pinion Drive · Arduino control

A forearm-mounted assistive device that translates muscle co-contraction into mechanical extension. Surface EMG signals are processed in real time to actuate a compact rack-and-pinion mechanism, converting biological intent into controlled linear motion.

CytoScope, 2023

Junior capstone project (ES96) · Optics · Stepper Motor · iPS cells

A low-cost, high-throughput imaging platform designed for the Harvard Stem Cell Institute iPS Core Facility. The system automates daily cell monitoring by integrating a compact brightfield microscope and motorized X‐Y‐Z stage directly inside a standard incubator.
